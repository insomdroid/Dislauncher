Dislauncher is a lightweight, text-first Home screen for Android. It keeps your favourite apps and everyday tools easy to reach without making icons or visual effects the focus.

Features:
- A calm Home screen with time, date, useful information and favourite apps.
- An app drawer with alphabetical browsing, favourites, folders, hidden apps and compatible icon packs.
- Personalisation options for themes, accent colours, wallpaper, fonts and text size.
- Built-in Notes with Markdown support, search, pinning, and import/export.
- A monthly Calendar and daily agenda for local tasks, reminders and optional device calendar events.
- A scientific Calculator and a Unit Converter.
- Optional weather, music controls, notification dots, shortcuts and gestures.

Privacy:
- No account is required.
- The reviewed source contains no advertising or analytics SDK.
- Notes, reminders and preferences are stored locally.
- Weather is optional and uses the Open-Meteo service when enabled.
- Permissions and special access are requested for features that need them.
