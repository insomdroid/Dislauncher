Dislauncher is a minimalist Android home-screen launcher designed to keep your phone simple, useful, and personal.

Features include:
- A clean, distraction-conscious home screen
- Customizable themes, colors, wallpapers, and app icons
- App drawer for accessing installed apps
- At-a-glance information such as weather, battery, network, and calendar agenda
- Built-in Notes, Calendar, Calculator, and Unit Converter tools
- Notification dots and music-related notification support
- Local reminders and optional access to device calendar events

Dislauncher is open source under the MIT license. Source code, release notes, and APK downloads are available on GitHub.
