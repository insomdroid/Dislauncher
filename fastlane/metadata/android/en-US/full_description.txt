Dislauncher is a minimalist, text-first Home screen for Android. It keeps app access simple while offering useful everyday tools without requiring an account.

FEATURES

• Minimal Home screen and app drawer
• Customise themes, accent colours, fonts, icons and wallpapers
• Built-in Notes with rich-text editing, search, pinning, Markdown import/export and portable archives
• Monthly Calendar and agenda combining local tasks/reminders with optional device calendar events
• Calculator and unit converter
• Optional weather, media controls, notification dots, app shortcuts and gestures

PRIVACY

• No account required
• No advertising or analytics SDKs identified in the reviewed source
• Notes, reminders and preferences are stored locally
• Calendar and notification access are optional
• Weather is optional; when enabled, approximate coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo over HTTPS
• Automatic Android backup is disabled, so export important notes before uninstalling or changing devices

Dislauncher is free and open-source software. Source code, release notes and issue reporting are available at:
https://github.com/insomdroid/Dislauncher
