The idea

Your phone should work for you, not compete for your attention.

Dislauncher puts useful information and clear text ahead of rows of oversized icons. Open apps quickly, organise the ones you rely on, and shape the experience around your routine.

01 / HOME

A calmer Home screen

Clock, date, progress indicators and favourite apps, with optional battery, weather, calendar agenda, music controls and notification indicators.

02 / APP ACCESS

Less searching. More doing.

Searchable alphabetical app drawer, favourites, folders, hidden apps, icon-pack support and shortcuts for common actions.

03 / PERSONALISATION

Your setup, your rules

System, light, dark and black themes, accent colours, wallpapers, a choice of fonts, text sizing, gestures and layout preferences.

NotesRich text, lists, checklists and images
CalendarMonthly view, daily agenda and reminders
CalculatorCalculations with recent history
Unit converterCommon units and measurements
Weather & musicOptional Home screen tools
04 / NOTES

Notes that keep up

Write with rich-text formatting, headings, links, lists, checklists and images. Search and pin notes, switch between list and grid views, and import or export notes for safekeeping.

05 / CALENDAR

See the day ahead

Browse the month, choose a date and see its agenda. Keep local tasks and reminders alongside events from your device calendar when you choose to connect them.

06 / EVERYDAY TOOLS

Useful when you need them

Work through calculations, revisit recent results, and convert common measurements without leaving your launcher experience.
